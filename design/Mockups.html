What this is. Every mod costs frame time whether you interact with it or not — hook chains fire on every tick regardless. This panel ranks mods by frames-paid-vs-engagement-observed. A mod at the top is costing CPU without (in this session) anything to show for it. No mod is removed automatically. The profiler reports; the player decides what's worth their slot.

Single-session signal only. A 0-engagement mod across one 2-hour session may be a different mod across 20 sessions — persistent storage (gated on Workshop policy) extends this from "today" to "ever".
